Sell or consign

Let something go, quietly.

Many pieces in our lookbook are offered on behalf of private clients. Your name is never published, your serials are never posted, and nothing is listed without your agreement in writing.

Sell outright

A written offer within two working days.

We make a firm offer after we have seen the piece. Payment by bank transfer the same day you accept.

Consign

Shown privately to the right buyer.

We authenticate, photograph, and present the piece to clients we know. The commission is agreed in writing before anything else happens.

How it works

  1. 01

    Tell us about it

    Use the form below or call. Photographs help but are not required.

  2. 02

    A first opinion

    Within one working day, a host replies with a candid view of what is possible.

  3. 03

    An appointment

    We see the piece in the salon or at your home, open it, and test it.

  4. 04

    Your decision

    A written offer or consignment terms. Take as long as you need.
