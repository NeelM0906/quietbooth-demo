Demonstration notice

Demonstration site. Pieces, people, and addresses are fictional. Product and editorial images are real photographs used under free licences; see the image credits. Pieces marked as reference are held by museums or were exhibited publicly. No piece shown here is for sale, and no request made on this site is sent anywhere. Requests are stored only in your own browser so that the flow can be demonstrated.

Privacy

We collect only what you give us: your name, contact details, and what you tell us about your visit or your piece. We use it to arrange appointments, answer enquiries, and, if you ask, send the private client letter.

We do not sell or share your details, run advertising trackers, or use cookies for marketing. Identity details required by law for larger purchases are kept securely for the period the law requires and no longer.

To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, write to concierge@quietbooth.example.

Terms of sale

Prices are given in writing and are valid for seven days unless agreed otherwise. A piece is reserved once a deposit is received and sold once paid in full. Pieces bought in the salon may be returned unworn within fourteen days in the condition they left. Custom work and resized rings are not returnable.

Every piece is sold with our lifetime guarantee of authenticity, described on the Authenticity page.

Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It works with a keyboard and a screen reader, respects your reduced motion setting, and keeps text contrast at or above 4.5 to 1. The salon is step-free from the lobby by elevator. If anything gets in your way, call +1 212 555 0147 and we will help.
