The House

Fourteen floors above the street, since 1987.

QuietBooth began as a single setting bench and a promise that no client would ever be rushed. The bench is still there. So is the promise.

History

  1. 1987

    Miriam Adler rents a single room on the fourteenth floor and sets stones for other jewellers by day, for private clients by night.

  2. 1996

    The first watches arrive, consigned by clients who wanted them sold quietly. They still are.

  3. 2011

    Daniel Okafor joins from the bench and begins writing the condition notes that accompany every watch.

  4. 2016

    Lena Voronova opens the private client service for collectors who buy across time zones.

  5. Today

    Three hosts, one room, around two hundred pieces a year, and no sign on the door.

What we hold to

Discretion

We do not photograph clients, publish sales, or name sellers. What happens in the room stays in the room.

Proof

Every claim on this site is backed by a document you can hold: a laboratory report, a service record, a set of measurements.

Patience

Nothing here is on sale and nothing expires tonight. If a piece is right for you, it will still be right next week.

Your hosts

You will always know who you are speaking to.

MA

Miriam Adler

Founder, high jewelry · since 1987

Miriam opened the salon after twelve years setting stones for houses on Fifth Avenue. She still inspects every diamond under the loupe before it is offered, and she will tell you when a stone is not the one.

Speaks English, Hebrew, French

DO

Daniel Okafor

Watches and provenance · since 2011

Daniel trained as a watchmaker before moving to the other side of the table. He opens every case, checks every serial against its papers, and writes the condition notes you read on this site.

Speaks English, Spanish

LV

Lena Voronova

Private clients · since 2016

Lena looks after collectors who buy across time zones. She arranges viewings, discreet delivery, and the paperwork that follows a serious piece home.
