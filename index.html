A private appointment

Three steps, and none of them rushed.

  1. I

    Choose a time

    Pick a morning or afternoon that suits you and tell us what you would like to see. We reply within one working day, usually sooner.

  2. II

    We prepare the room

    Your host sets out the pieces you asked about, with their reports and condition notes, and one or two we think you should see.

  3. III

    Visit, unhurried

    One guest party at a time, about an hour, daylight at the table. Decide there, or take the dossier home and decide later.

Founder, high jewelry · since 1987

Miriam opened the salon after twelve years setting stones for houses on Fifth Avenue. She still inspects every diamond under the loupe before it is offered, and she will tell you when a stone is not the one.
