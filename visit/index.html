On arrival

  1. I

    The lobby

    Give your name and photo identification at the front desk. They will have it on their list from your confirmation.

  2. II

    The fourteenth floor

    Take the right-hand elevators and turn left. Suite 1400 is the unmarked walnut door at the end of the corridor.

  3. III

    The bell

    Ring once. Your host opens the door personally. The outer door locks behind you, which is normal and for your security.

Discretion and security

We never photograph clients, post visits, or share our appointment book. The building has round-the-clock security, and the salon is monitored inside and out.

If you would rather not be seen arriving, ask for the side entrance on West 48th Street when you book, and your host will meet you there. For larger purchases we can arrange armoured delivery, or a viewing at your home, office, or hotel.

If you can, avoid wearing visible jewelry on the street on your way to us. It is a small precaution the whole block takes seriously.
