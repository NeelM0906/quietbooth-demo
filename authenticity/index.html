Authenticity and provenance

We would rather lose a piece than explain one later.

Buyers on 47th Street are right to worry about replaced parts, refinished dials, and stones that are not what the paper says. This is what we do about it, every time.

Watches

Six checks before a watch is photographed

  1. 01

    Open the case

    Under a dust cover, by a watchmaker. Calibre and serial are compared to the case and to the papers.

  2. 02

    Read the dial

    Under ten times magnification for printing relief, lume consistency, and signs of refinishing.

  3. 03

    Measure the case

    Lug thickness and bevels compared to factory references to judge polishing honestly.

  4. 04

    Check the parts

    Hands, crown, crystal, and bracelet are matched to the reference and the year.

  5. 05

    Time it

    Twenty-four hours on a timing machine in five positions. Results go in the dossier.

  6. 06

    Write it down

    Plain-language condition notes, including what a seller might prefer to leave out.

Diamonds and coloured stones

Four checks before a stone is set

  1. 01

    Independent report

    Every significant stone carries a report from an independent gemmological laboratory. We do not grade our own stones.

  2. 02

    Inscription check

    Where a girdle inscription exists, we match it to the report under the loupe.

  3. 03

    Origin and treatment

    Coloured stones are reported for origin and treatment. We say clearly when a stone is enhanced.

  4. 04

    Daylight viewing

    We judge colour by a north window and a daylight lamp, never under display spots.

A guarantee without an expiry date.

If any piece we sell is ever shown not to be what we described, we will refund the full price you paid. There is no time limit, and the guarantee passes to the next owner with the dossier.

We will not sell watches with replaced dials described as original, stones without a report where one is customary, or anything whose ownership we cannot trace.

The dossier

What you take home

  • Photographs of the movement or stone
  • Case measurements and timing results
  • Laboratory reports, original and translated
  • Service history we have verified
  • Replaced parts, sealed and labelled
  • A signed letter of authenticity
