The story

From 1988 to 2000 the automatic Daytona ran on calibre 4030, Rolex's heavily reworked version of the Zenith El Primero. Collectors call these the Zenith Daytonas, and the yellow gold 16528 is the most luminous of them.

This example carries an S-prefix serial, which places it in the early 1990s. The white dial with black registers reads cleanly against the gold.
