The story

The Lumen was drawn as a watch for every day of a long life. At 38 millimetres it sits close to the wrist, and the slate dial moves from charcoal to silver as the light turns.

This example came to us from its first owner, a physician in Boston, with the full set. The case has never been polished. You can still see the factory brushing on the lugs.
