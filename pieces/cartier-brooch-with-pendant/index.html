The story

Cartier's Art Deco brooches traded the garlands of the Belle Époque for open geometry: frames, steps, and triangles of diamonds set in platinum-white mounts.

This brooch suspends a pearl from a diamond triangle beneath an open rectangular frame. It was shown in the Jazz Age exhibition at the Cleveland Museum of Art.

Provenance

  • Exhibited: "Jazz Age: American Style in the 1920s", Cleveland Museum of Art
