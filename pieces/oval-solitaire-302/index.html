The story

Miriam looked at forty oval diamonds to choose this one. The proportions keep the bow tie to a whisper, and the stone faces up larger than its weight would suggest.

The band is hand-forged and set with four claws, low enough to slip under a glove. We can resize it in our workshop within a week.
