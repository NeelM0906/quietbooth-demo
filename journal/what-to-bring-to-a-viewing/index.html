What to bring to a private viewing

A short, practical note for anyone coming to see us for the first time.

By Lena Voronova, private clients

A first appointment is unhurried. Most guests stay for about an hour. You do not need to know exactly what you want, but a few things make the time more useful.

Bring what you wear

If you are choosing a watch, wear the one you wear most. It tells us about your wrist, your habits, and your taste faster than any conversation. For jewellery, wear what you would wear with it: a ring you will stack, the neckline you love.

Bring a question you are unsure about

Every guest has one. Whether a stone is too large, whether a vintage watch is practical, whether something is worth what it costs. Ask it early. We would rather talk you out of a piece than sell you one you doubt.

The best appointments end with a decision or with a reason to wait, and both are fine.

Bring identification

The building asks every visitor for photo identification at the front desk. This is for everyone's security, and the name is held only by the building and by us.

If you prefer not to be seen arriving, tell us. We can meet you at the side entrance or arrange a viewing elsewhere in the city.
