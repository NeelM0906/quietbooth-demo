Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Appointments

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. The salon is not open to walk-in visitors. Appointments keep the room quiet and let us prepare the pieces you want to see, so nothing you came for is out on another table.

How long is a viewing?

Usually one hour. If you would like more time, or want to compare several stones, say so when you request and we will hold the room longer.

Can I bring someone?

Of course. Up to four guests in total. Please include their names so the building can expect them.

Can you come to me?

For serious enquiries we can arrange a viewing at your home, office, or hotel within Manhattan, accompanied by one of our hosts and building security.

Pieces and pricing

Why are prices not listed?

Many of our pieces are one of one, and some are offered on behalf of private clients who prefer discretion. We will always give you a clear price in writing before you visit, if you ask.

Are the pieces authentic?

Every watch is opened and checked against its papers, and every significant stone carries an independent laboratory report. You can read our full process on the Authenticity page.

Can a piece be held for me?

We can hold a piece for forty-eight hours while you arrange a viewing. Longer holds are possible with a small refundable deposit.

Do you resize and service?

Rings are resized in our workshop. Watches are serviced by independent watchmakers we have worked with for years.

Payment and delivery

How can I pay?

Bank transfer, certified cheque, or card for smaller pieces. We do not accept cash above the legal reporting limit, and we follow all applicable identity checks.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Insured delivery by armoured courier, or by a host in person within the city. International shipping is fully insured and arranged case by case.

What if I change my mind?

Pieces bought in the salon may be returned unworn within fourteen days, in the condition they left. Custom work is not returnable.
